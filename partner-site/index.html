A pretend partner website for the AFTERLIFE demo. Acme Book Club is made up. It is hosted separately from Shelf (on GitLab Pages) and embeds Shelf's “currently reading” widget the way a real partner would. Nothing is sent until you click: then it makes one request to /embed/widget with Acme's partner key. While Shelf holds a vigil for the widget, that request is a knock and brings the widget back.
